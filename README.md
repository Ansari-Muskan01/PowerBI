# HR Analytics 

**Project Objective**

The objective of this project is to analyze employee data and provide meaningful insights into workforce structure, employee performance, salary, engagement, satisfaction, attrition, experience, training, promotion, and work-life balance.

The Power BI report will help HR teams understand:

Total number of employees
Employee distribution across departments, locations, and job roles
Salary and compensation patterns
Employee performance
Employee engagement and satisfaction
Attrition patterns
Employee experience and tenure
Training and promotion trends
Work-life balance
Remote work and overtime
Department and job role level workforce trends

The report will help HR teams identify important workforce patterns and areas that may require attention.
